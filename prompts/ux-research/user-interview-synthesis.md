## Synthesize User Interview Notes

**Goal:** Quickly extract themes, pain points, and opportunities from raw interview notes.
**Tool:** ChatGPT / Claude
**Design Phase:** Discovery

### Prompt

> I conducted user interviews and have the following raw notes. Please:
> 1. Identify the top 5 recurring themes.
> 2. List the key pain points with supporting quotes.
> 3. Suggest 3 design opportunities based on the patterns.
>
> Raw notes:
> [PASTE YOUR NOTES HERE]

### Notes

- Works best when notes are pasted in a consistent format (one participant per block).
- Follow up with: *"Now prioritize these opportunities by user impact vs. implementation effort."*
