# AI-Assisted Competitive Analysis

**Overview:** Use AI to rapidly scan and synthesize competitor products before a design sprint.
**Tools needed:** ChatGPT or Claude, FigJam or Miro, your browser.
**Time estimate:** 2–3 hours (vs. a full day manually).
**Output:** A structured comparison table + opportunity gaps ready for your team.

---

## Steps

### 1. Define Your Lens (15 min)

Decide what dimensions matter for your product. Common lenses:
- Onboarding experience
- Core task flow efficiency
- Information architecture
- Visual language & tone
- Accessibility

### 2. Prompt AI for a First-Pass Overview (20 min)

Use this prompt for each competitor:

> *"Describe [COMPETITOR NAME]'s product from a UX perspective. Focus on: their primary user flows, notable UI patterns, tone of voice, and any known usability strengths or criticisms. Cite specific features where possible."*

### 3. Screenshot & Annotate (60 min)

Manually capture key screens. Use AI to help annotate:

> *"Here is a screenshot of [COMPETITOR]'s checkout flow. List the UX patterns used and flag any friction points."*

### 4. Synthesize into a Comparison Table (30 min)

> *"I have analyzed 4 competitors: [A, B, C, D]. Here are my notes for each: [PASTE]. Create a comparison table across these dimensions: [your lens from Step 1]. Then identify 3 whitespace opportunities."*

### 5. Share with Your Team

Drop the table into FigJam. Run a 30-min "How Might We" session using the opportunity gaps as seeds.

---

## Tips

- Don't rely solely on AI descriptions — always validate against the live product.
- Use the same prompt structure for each competitor so outputs are easier to compare.
- Version this document when you revisit the competitive landscape.
